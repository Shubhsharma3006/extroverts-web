Terms & Conditions

Last updated August 3, 2026

1. Acceptance of terms

By creating an account on Extroverts, you agree to these Terms & Conditions and our Privacy Policy. If you don't agree, please don't create an account or use the app.

2. Who can join

You must be at least 18 years old to create an Extroverts account. Meeting up with people you've matched with online carries real-world risk, and we restrict the platform to adults for everyone's safety. We may ask you to confirm your age again later, and we'll close accounts we find belong to anyone under 18.

3. Your account

  • You're responsible for keeping your login details private.
  • One account per person — no impersonating someone else, and no fake profiles.
  • You agree that the name, age, and other details you provide are accurate.
  • We may suspend or remove accounts that violate these terms, without prior notice.

4. Community conduct

Extroverts exists to help people meet in the real world, so we hold the community to a real-world standard of respect:

  • No harassment, hate speech, or threatening behavior, in the app or at events.
  • No soliciting money, selling products, or running scams through the app.
  • Be honest about who's hosting an event and what it actually involves.
  • Respect other members' boundaries, both online and in person.

5. Events & in-person meetups

Extroverts is a platform for discovering and organizing meetups — we are not present at, and do not supervise, any event. You're responsible for your own safety and decisions when attending. We recommend meeting new people in public places, telling a friend your plans, and trusting your judgment.

6. Privacy & your data

We collect the information you provide during signup (like your email, name, age, and location) along with device identifiers, to run the app and keep the community safe. We encrypt your data in transit and never sell it to third parties. You can request deletion of your data at any time from Settings, or by contacting us.

7. Content you share

You keep ownership of photos and posts you share, but you give Extroverts permission to display them within the app so other members can see your profile and events. Don't post anything you don't have the right to share.

8. Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings. We may suspend or terminate accounts that violate these terms, engage in unsafe behavior, or misuse the platform.

9. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as the app evolves. If we make material changes, we'll let you know in the app before they take effect.

10. Contact us

Questions about these terms? Reach us at hello@extroverts.app.
